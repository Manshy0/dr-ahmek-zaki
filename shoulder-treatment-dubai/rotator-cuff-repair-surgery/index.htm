What Is Rotator Cuff Repair Surgery?

Rotator cuff repair surgery is a procedure that reattaches torn rotator cuff tendons back to the bone. The rotator cuff, four muscles and tendons stabilizing your shoulder, can tear from injury, repetitive strain, or degeneration, causing pain, weakness, and functional loss. As a specialist rotator cuff surgeon in Dubai, Dr. Ahmed Zaki performs arthroscopic rotator cuff surgery through 3-4 tiny incisions using a high-definition camera. The rotator cuff repair technique secures the torn tendon to bone with strong suture anchors, allowing biological healing over 3-6 months. This same-day outpatient shoulder rotator cuff surgery replaces the need for large open incisions, hospital stays, or prolonged recovery.

When Is Rotator Cuff Repair Surgery Needed?

Not every rotator cuff tear requires rotator cuff surgery. Dr. Ahmed Zaki evaluates each patient carefully before recommending surgery.

Surgery Typically Recommended When:

Conservative treatment failed after 3-6 months · Complete full-thickness tears · Large/massive tears (3cm+) · Acute tear in active individual · Significant weakness affecting daily life · Progressive tear enlargement on imaging · Persistent pain despite all treatments · Occupation or sport requires full overhead strength

→ Early surgery = better tissue quality & outcomes

Surgery May NOT Be Needed When:

Partial-thickness tears responding to therapy · Small tears (<1cm) in less active patients · First attempt, conservative care should be tried · Very chronic tears with severe fatty infiltration · Significant medical conditions making surgery high-risk · Symptoms tolerable and function acceptable at current activity level

→ Dr. Ahmed Zaki always recommends conservative first

Evaluation covers: tear size and location on MRI, muscle quality and fatty infiltration (Goutallier grading), physical strength and ROM testing, your activity goals and occupation, response to previous treatments, smoking status, and overall health.

How Is Rotator Cuff Repair Surgery Performed?

Understanding how is rotator cuff surgery performed reduces anxiety and sets realistic expectations. Dr. Ahmed Zaki performs arthroscopic shoulder surgery rotator cuff repair in 95%+ of cases, through 3-4 tiny incisions with high-definition visualization.

Step 1 - Anaesthesia & Positioning (15-20 min)

General anaesthesia + interscalene nerve block numbing shoulder 12-18hrs post-op. Beach-chair or lateral positioning. Surgical site marked and sterilized.

Step 2 - Portal Creation & Diagnostic Arthroscopy (10-15 min)

3-4 small incisions created. Arthroscope inserted for systematic HD inspection of the rotator cuff tear, bicep tendon, labrum, and cartilage surfaces. Confirms surgical plan.

Step 3 - Joint Preparation (15-20 min)

Inflamed bursa removed. Bone spurs shaved (subacromial decompression). Frayed tear edges debrided. Bone footprint roughened, the bleeding surface is critical for suture anchor rotator cuff repair biological healing.

Step 4 - Rotator Cuff Repair Technique (30-45 min)

Tendon mobilized. Suture anchors inserted into bone (2-8 anchors depending on tear size). High-strength sutures passed through tendon and tied using arthroscopic knot-tying. Single-row or double-row rotator cuff repair technique used based on tear.

Step 5 - Associated Pathology (10-20 min if needed)

Bicep tendon treated (tenotomy or tenodesis). Labral tears repaired. AC joint arthritis treated if indicated. All pathology addressed in one arthroscopic rotator cuff surgery session.

Step 6 - Closure (10 min)

Final inspection confirms secure repair and full passive motion. Incisions closed with absorbable sutures or surgical glue. Waterproof dressings applied. Arm placed in immobilisation sling for home.

Risks & Possible Complications

Dr. Ahmed Zaki believes in full transparency about rotator cuff surgery risks and complications. Overall serious complication rate is under 3-5% for arthroscopic repairs by experienced surgeons.

Recovery Timeline Overview:

Rotator Cuff Repair Failure Rate (5-20%)

Tendon may fail to heal to bone or re-tear after healing. Higher with large/massive tears, smokers (smoking doubles re-tear risk), poorly controlled diabetes, age over 65, non-compliance with restrictions.

Rotator Cuff Surgery Infection (<1%)

Rare with arthroscopic technique and sterile protocols. Minimized through prophylactic antibiotics and antibacterial skin preparation. Treated with antibiotics.

Stiffness / Frozen Shoulder (5-10%)

Post-operative stiffness from scar tissue. More common in diabetics. Prevented through early physical therapy. Treated with aggressive stretching or, rarely, manipulation under anaesthesia

Rotator Cuff Surgery Complications Minimised By:

Careful patient selection · Meticulous 23-year arthroscopic technique · Mandatory smoking cessation · Diabetes pre-op optimization · Structured rehab protocol · Close post-op follow-up

Recovery & Rehabilitation After Rotator Cuff Repair Surgery

Rotator cuff repair recovery follows a structured 4-phase protocol. Rotator cuff surgery recovery time depends on tear size, tissue quality, age, and rehabilitation compliance.

Phase-1 Protection

Weeks 0-6

Sling immobilisation non-negotiable. Passive ROM only. Sleeping after rotator cuff surgery requires reclined position. Nerve block provides initial pain control.

Phase-2 Active Motion

Weeks 6-12

Sling weaning begins. Active-assisted ROM introduced. Light isometric strengthening. Return to desk work typically possible. Return to work after rotator cuff surgery for office roles.

Phase-3 Strengthening

Weeks 12-20

Progressive resistance exercises. Rotator cuff surgery rehab protocol advances to functional and sport-specific work. Rotator cuff repair physical therapy intensive. Light manual work possible.

Phase-4 Full Return

Weeks 20-24+

Advanced conditioning. Return to sports after rotator cuff surgery cleared at 90%+ strength. Full recovery 6-12 months depending on tear size and individual factors.

Frequently asked questions

Need something cleared up? Here are our most frequently asked questions.

How long does rotator cuff surgery take?

Most arthroscopic rotator cuff repairs take 60-90 minutes for small to medium tears, 90-120 minutes for large or massive tears, and 120-150 minutes for complex revision cases. Total time at the surgical centre including preparation and recovery monitoring is approximately 3-4 hours before same-day discharge

What is the rotator cuff surgery success rate?

Rotator cuff surgery success rate ranges from 90-95% for small tears to 65-80% for massive tears. Non-smokers with good tissue quality consistently achieve the highest healing rates. Dr. Ahmed Zaki's fellowship training and arthroscopic expertise achieve outcomes at the top of published success rate ranges for all tear sizes.

How long is rotator cuff surgery recovery time?

Rotator cuff surgery recovery time: sling at 4-6 weeks, desk work at 2-6 weeks, full unrestricted activities at 6-12 months. How long rotator cuff surgery recovery takes depends on tear size, tissue quality, age, and rehabilitation compliance. Dr. Ahmed Zaki provides a personalised rotator cuff surgery recovery timeline at consultation.

Is rotator cuff surgery worth it?

For appropriately selected patients: yes. 85-95% achieve significant pain relief, most regain 85-100% of normal strength, 90%+ return to prior activities, and 90-95% report they would do it again. Rotator cuff surgery is worth it when you have a repairable tear, have given conservative treatment a fair trial, and your function demands a strong shoulder. Dr. Ahmed Zaki ensures you are the right candidate before recommending surgery.

What does rotator cuff surgery cost in Dubai? Is it covered by insurance?

Rotator cuff surgery cost in Dubai ranges from AED 30,000-60,000 for standard arthroscopic repairs. Rotator cuff surgery insurance coverage applies when medically necessary most major UAE insurers including Daman, DHA, Cigna, AXA, and Bupa cover it. Our team verifies your rotator cuff surgery insurance coverage and obtains pre-authorisation before surgery.

When can I drive after rotator cuff surgery?

Driving after rotator cuff surgery is possible at 2-4 weeks for left shoulder (automatic car), 4-6 weeks for right shoulder. You must be off narcotic medications, out of the sling, and have full arm control to steer and react safely. Dr. Ahmed Zaki provides individual clearance based on your recovery progress.

What about sleeping after rotator cuff surgery?

Sleeping after rotator cuff surgery requires a reclined position for 4-6 weeks a recliner is ideal, otherwise use a wedge pillow at 45 degrees. Lying flat increases pressure on the repair and pain. Ice every 2 hours for 20 minutes significantly reduces swelling during early recovery.

Can elderly patients or those over 60 have rotator cuff surgery?

Yes. Rotator cuff surgery over 60 and rotator cuff surgery elderly patients achieve excellent outcomes age alone is not a contraindication. Success rates are slightly lower for very large tears in elderly patients due to tissue quality, but pain relief and functional improvement are well-documented across all ages. Dr. Ahmed Zaki evaluates each case individually.

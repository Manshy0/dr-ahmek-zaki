    Expert care for ligament tears, joint degeneration, and knee reconstruction.

    What I Treat
    ACL & Meniscus Tears Knee Osteoarthritis & Degeneration Patellar Tendonitis (Jumper's Knee) Knee Instability & Ligament Injuries
    How I Treat

    Sports Injuries

    Specialized recovery plans to return athletes to peak performance.

    What I Treat
    Muscle Strains & Ligament Sprains Chronic Tendonitis & Overuse Labral Tears (Shoulder/Hip) Runner’s Knee & Shin Splints Tennis & Golfer’s Elbow
    How I Treat
    Sports Physical Therapy & Rehab Minimally Invasive Sports Surgery Biological Repair (PRP/Stem Cell) Joint Stabilization Procedures Elbow Arthroscopy

    Fracture Trauma Care

    Immediate and reconstructive care for complex bone injuries and trauma.

    What I Treat
    Complex & Compound Fractures Non-Union & Mal-Union Fractures Stress Fractures Dislocated Joints
    How I Treat
    Internal Fixation (Plates/Screws) Minimally Invasive Fracture Repair Trauma Reconstruction Surgery Casting, Bracing, and Splinting

    Hip, Back & Spine

    Expert management for spinal discs, sciatica, and hip impingement.

    What I Treat
    Herniated Discs & Sciatica Hip Impingement (FAI) & Bursitis Spinal Stenosis & Disc Disease Hip Labral Tears
    How I Treat
    Minimally Invasive Spine Surgery Total Hip Replacement Spinal Injections (Epidurals) Hip Arthroscopy

    Non-Surgical Orthopedic

    Advanced regenerative therapies and conservative treatment options.

    Chronic Joint Pain (Arthritis) Soft Tissue Inflammation Early-Stage Tendon Degeneration Work-Related Repetitive Strain
    How I Treat
    PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) Therapy Viscosupplementation (Gel Injections) Shockwave Therapy (ESWT) Custom Orthotics & Correction

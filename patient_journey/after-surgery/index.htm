Recovery That Restores Your Strength

A comprehensive guide to your orthopedic surgery recovery in the UAE. Dr. Ahmed Zaki is with you every step of the way to ensure a smooth, confident, and complete return to strength.

What Happens Right After Surgery?

Monitored in the recovery room
Immediate pain management begins
Sterile wound dressing applied
Guided first steps or arm movements
Discharge instructions and contact details provided

The 3 Phases of Recovery

Early Recovery
(0–2 Weeks)

Rest, wound care, light walking or passive motion

Phase 2: Rehabilitation
(2–6 Weeks)

Guided physio for strength, flexibility, and range

Phase 3: Long-Term Healing
(6 Weeks+)

Build muscle, return to sports, restore confidence

Pain Management & Comfort

Personalized medications
Cold packs for inflammation
Emotional reassurance
24/7 team contact for urgent concerns

“We don’t just manage pain     we manage peace of mind.”

Movement Is Medicine

Gentle movement speeds healing and prevents complications. Your therapy plan is never copy-paste it’s designed.

When to Call Us After Surgery

Fever over 38°C

Shortness of breath

Redness, pus, or unusual pain

No improvement in swelling or motion

Always reach out. It’s better to check than to worry.

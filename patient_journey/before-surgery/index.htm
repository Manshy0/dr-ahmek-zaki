Appointment

Your Surgery Starts with Confidence

A step-by-step guide to your orthopedic surgery preparation in the UAE. We ensure you are fully informed and confident before your procedure with Dr. Ahmed Zaki.

A Clear Roadmap to Surgery

Diagnosis & Decision

You and Dr. Ahmed Zaki agree that surgery is the best path forward.

Meeting Your Anesthesiologist

You will meet with the anesthesiologist before your surgery to ensure a safe preparation and to discuss the anesthesia options that best suit your case.

Preoperative Assessment

Blood work, imaging, ECG, and other tests will be arranged to confirm surgical readiness.

One-on-One Pre-Surgery Consultation

A detailed conversation about the surgery, expectations, benefits, and potential risks.

Pre-Surgery Instructions

Guidance on fasting, medications, showering, and what to bring to the hospital.

Final Prep Call (Day Before Surgery)

Our team confirms arrival time, fasting instructions, and answers any last-minute questions.

Your Pre-Surgery Checklist

[ 01 ]

Complete all medical tests as instructed

[ 02 ]

Inform Dr. Ahmed Zaki about all medications / supplements

[ 03 ]

Stop blood-thinning medications if directed

[ 04 ]

Follow fasting rules (typically 6–8 hours before surgery)

[ 05 ]

Arrange for transportation to/from the hospital

[ 06 ]

Pack comfortable clothes and essentials

[ 07 ]

Prepare your home for post-surgery recovery

[ 08 ]

Bring all documents, reports, and imaging

Proactive recommendations pre-surgery

Eat well

Prioritize balanced meals and hydration in the weeks before.

Stay active

Light, doctor-approved exercise helps with healing and stamina.

Stay calm

Deep breathing, prayer, or meditation can reduce stress and improve outcomes.

Oral medication

In some cases, oral medication is given before surgery to help manage post-operative pain

What Happens the Night Before Surgery?

  • Follow your fasting instructions strictly.
  • Shower with antiseptic soap if advised.
  • Avoid lotions or makeup.
  • Sleep early rest helps your body prepare.

A Trusted Name in Orthopedic & Trauma Surgery

Dr. Ahmed Zaki is a board-certified orthopedic and trauma surgeon, specializing in minimally invasive and joint replacement surgeries for the shoulder, knee, elbow, wrist, and hip. With training from world-class institutions like the University of Toronto, he brings international expertise combined with genuine care to every patient he treats.

He holds an MD, PhD, AO Trauma (UK), and is a fellowship-trained specialist in Trauma & Sports Injuries. Beyond his credentials, what truly defines Dr. Ahmed Zaki is his belief that every patient deserves personalized attention, transparent communication, and a treatment plan built around their lifestyle.

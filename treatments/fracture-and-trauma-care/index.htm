Swelling, bruising, or protruding bone
Numbness or tingling post-injury

Fracture & Trauma FAQs

Need something cleared up? Here are our most frequently asked questions.

Do all fractures require surgery?

No. Many fractures can heal with bracing or casting depending on type, displacement, and location.

How soon should I get a fracture checked?

Immediately. Even a minor fracture can become worse if left untreated.

What if I already had surgery elsewhere and it didn’t heal well?

Dr. Ahmed Zaki specializes in revision fracture surgeries and delayed union/malunion corrections.

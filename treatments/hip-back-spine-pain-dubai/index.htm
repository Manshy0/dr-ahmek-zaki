Don’t Let Spine or Hip Pain Disrupt Your Life

Back and hip pain are among the most common complaints in daily orthopedic practice. While Dr. Ahmed Zaki focuses on upper limb and joint care, he regularly helps patients navigate the first and most crucial step—accurate diagnosis and non-surgical management of musculoskeletal spine and hip issues. In many cases, conservative treatments and guided rehab can prevent or delay surgery.

Conditions We Assess and Help Manage

Hip:

  • Hip impingement (FAI) 

  • Bursitis 

  • Early arthritis 

  • Tendonitis or muscular imbalance

Back & Spine:

  • Lumbar strain and postural pain 

  • Disc bulges with mild symptoms 

  • Sciatica (conservative stage) 

  • Muscle spasms or SI joint irritation

Note: Surgical cases are referred to trusted spine and hip surgeons after full evaluation.

Common Questions About
Spine, Hip & Back Pain

Does all back pain require surgery?

No. In fact, most cases can be managed with posture correction, physical therapy, and targeted rehab.

Can PRP help my hip?

Yes. PRP is often used to treat early hip arthritis or tendon inflammation conservatively.

When should I see a doctor about back pain?

If it persists beyond 2 weeks, affects sleep, causes numbness/tingling, or interferes with daily life.
